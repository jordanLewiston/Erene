Euredius

A planet 1.7 times the width of Earth, ruled by a constitutional monarchy that shares power with a parliament and a church, and home to the people who speak Erene.

Symbols of the Kingdom

The flag, the royal seal and the passport of the Kingdom of Euredius.

Five regions

Euredius is a kingdom with a federal structure: in the east, each state runs itself. Every region has its own script, dialect and economy.

North (Dhoresa)

The palace, the main church, the central bank and the whole government sit in one white-marble megalopolis of fine steel, stone and basalt. It is the world’s politics, finance and prestige, and the priciest region, though housing there is cheaper than in the west. Its script, Dhoresa, is the official script of the planet, and it looks like something between Greek and Latin.

Center (Dhorenheres)

Once only fields and villages, now the banking region. It borrows from every other region and copies northern dignity, though it is also heavily industrialized. Housing is cheaper toward the east and dearer toward the west. Its script resembles the northern one with some letters left out or written differently.

South (Narhael)

The industrial heartland, where workers produce almost everything but food. Its metal-and-stone architecture is considered finer than the north’s, which makes it known as the architecture heaven. Prices are affordable. Its script looks like hiragana with many dots.

West (Franes)

Forests, rivers and marble shaped by nature itself, with few skyscrapers and little infrastructure beyond the shared banking system. Environmentalists, healers and sailors live here. The western dialect is the sailors’ dialect.

East (Demoelor)

The shadow economy: gangs, warlords and black markets run in plain daylight, and it looks more civilized than its reputation suggests. Prices are low. Criminals are sent here, and the rest of the planet largely ignores it. Its script resembles Arabic.

Dialects follow the regions. Northern is the general one, found in media, books and documents. Western is the sailors’ dialect, southern the industrial one, and eastern the criminal one.

The capital

The main church stands at Bralierandio 17, left of Examirion Avenue. The palace is on Qwarilio Alley. Orandion 253 holds the Legion’s plaza; the Legion is something like the Avengers of Euredius, with no superpowers.

On Orandion Street you will find the buffet of Mrs Memrien, famous for muffins. People also swear by the cupcakes of Mrs Eleera. The subway is called awarwa, a metro station is awareztexa, and stops include Bralieio and Nessa Magdanilien.

How Euredius is governed

A constitutional monarchy with a double-lock approval system, political pluralism and a parasecular government. Law passes only when two of three bodies agree.

Parliament

Handles the day-to-day tasks of government.

Its laws need the approval of the palace and the church.

The Palace

Handles matters of high caliber.

Its laws need the approval of the church and parliament.

The Church

Acts as the ethics committee.

It can only approve. It can never issue a law.

When the nation rejects a law

  1. The people choose a representative, who must be at least a junior-partner-level lawyer.
  2. The representative writes a manifesto.
  3. The manifesto goes to both the palace and the constitutional court.
  4. The constitutional court has the last word.

The planet is one kingdom, yet each state, especially in the east, has its own government, rather like the states of a federal country.

The northern legal system is about 90% similar to the United States’, with a 10% difference. The south leans toward civil law with a touch of communism.

Euredius has diplomatic conflicts with other planets over diplomacy and business. It ranks first in the galaxy for its economy, second for its judicial system, seventh for health care and third for social security. It has the second-largest military. It ranks 798th in the xenophobia rankings.

Exile is a real punishment. Exiles who go through a portal are reborn as infants on another planet while keeping their old souls. Those who do not are sent to the nearest moon and must find a home there.

Economy

A semi-free market: partly planned, partly open. The state provides basic food, housing, education and health care for the poor, while commerce and luxury are pure capitalism.

History

Writing began 171,751 years ago. The modern civilization, counted from colonization, is about 200,000 years old.

  • 279 million years agoEuredius’s evolutionary history begins. Civilization itself appears about 2.5 million years ago.
  • The old planet, RassaBefore colonization, Rassa is home to witches, pagans, vagrants and nature people who live in peace and rely on spells and cosmic powers.
  • About 200,000 years ago: colonizationThe galactic senate of the Daron Empire, whose original planet was Dauradius, colonizes Rassa and renames it Euredius. Most inhabitants refuse to change their culture and are eliminated.
  • The first kingSevere purges of the pagans and witches.
  • The second and third kingsThe persecutions stop. The third king gives the west to the environmentalists, hippies and witches, and concentrates power in the north.
  • The fourth kingExiles the rebels to the east instead of purging them.
  • The south is foundExplorers find huge deposits of ordinary industrial metal. The king concentrates the workforce there. The center, meanwhile, is still fields and villages, and becomes a banking region later.
  • 171,751 years agoWriting history begins.
  • 171,717 years agoRalu lives and ascends (see Faith).
  • Dates unknownMala Hales XI becomes the first female monarch. The linguist Dhalhar merson Harles codifies Erene for the first time, because it had been only spoken before.

Some details of the early kings come from different tellings. Dates for the later events are not yet fixed.

Faith: Ralu and the Church

Ralu, whose name is also the number seventeen, is the almighty creator. He lived 171,717 years ago. He turned sand into glass with a touch, healed the mute, blind and deaf, raised the dead, and greened the eternal deserts with a prayer, so that rain fell as never before. Two horses, the eronien, once saved his life; the letter mare is written enclosed by two of them.

When he was 17, seven corrupted men beat him with the knout (narse) and threw him into the black flames of Doros. He ascended, the world shook, and their bloodlines were cursed with sickness, pain and poverty.

The scripture has 17 books of Hanor. Naem is the Euredi’s bible and Rahara is their religion. Families read the Book of Ralu at the supper table and sing the Narae, the songs of the faith.

A marriage is bound by the Narena, the bond that only death can separate. At a wedding the bride and groom cut their palms in church, clasp opposite hands so their blood mixes, and take their vows. Seventeen liters of strong wine are poured over their scars, and seventeen more when they kiss.

Leave thy praises to me, but fill thy heart with love for person next and thy family and thyself, and thank thy parents.From the Book of Ralu

Society and daily life

Two registers. Erene has a high register for nobles and a low register for commoners, and nothing in between. Using the high register as a commoner is not forbidden, but doing so a lot looks like showing off. Nobles speak to commoners in the low register, and commoners still recognize them by how they build their sentences. The two registers differ in vocabulary, not only in grammar.

Writing. Each region has its own script. Official letters always use the northern script, and personal letters use the script of the region they are sent to. The language has 34 letters plus 17 specifiers; official letters to the palace, parliament, church or a company must use them.

Lives. Nobles live roughly 517 to 1,037 Earth years, and everyone else up to about 270. People sleep 18 to 20 hours a day, and some sleep 23.

Time. A day is 51 hours (28 of daylight and 23 of night), an hour is 51 minutes, and a minute is 51 seconds. A month is 119 days and a year is 17 months. At the uninhabited poles, day and night each last 17 years.

Food. Ghamasso: potatoes bathed in a batter of eggs, salt, milk, sugar, mkala sauce and minced cheese, baked for up to two hours, served with grilled deer meat and sliced greens.

Culture. The Darleks are pet birds whose spread wings look like living kaleidoscopes. People like to watch the skyline stars from the 27th floor with Emalia Erzalina playing, while the Erkzerks melt the planet with death metal. The saying goes: Nesete lemre mares em plarete lemre ior, “flow like water and fall like birds”. Nessa is a girl’s name and means a green light.

Humor. An executioner was told to kill two people. Because the word for two sounds like the word for twenty, he executed twenty, and the authorities sent him back to grammar school.

Dangers. Do not swim in Arsont mara, the “death water”, unless you want to feed the hja algae and the lkan sharks. Long ago, the Euredi mixed with the antennaed Marhats.
