How sentences are built

Verbs live in the infinitive. Add -te to give a command or direction, or drop the final e before -te for the past tense.

Add -en to a countable noun to make it plural: laru (friend) becomes laruen.

Add -esse to build a noun, and -urdiame to turn a verb into a gerund.

Pronouns attach to the verb. Emue means “I am”, and ture means “you are” or “your”.

Endings called thesmo forms signal the speaker’s gender, the rank of the person you address and the register of the topic.

Words are built from roots. Naru (water) gives naruen, and mesmano (love) gives mesmane, “to love”.

Five regions, five scripts

Erene was spoken long before it was written. The linguist Dhalhar merson Harles first coded it. Each region of Euredius then grew its own alphabet.

Dhoresa

North. The capital, cosmopolitan and elite, and an intergalactic hub.

Dhorenheres

Center. Banks, and a culture that imitates the north.

Narhael

South. The industrial zone that makes what everyone uses.

Franes

West. Environmentalists, healers and alternative traditions.

Demoelor

East. Black markets and the underworld.

Learners choose between Latro Ereniel, Erene in Latin letters, and Oprie Ereniel, written in a native alphabet.

The world behind the words

Euredius is a planet 1.7 times the size of Earth. Its people, the Euredi, once mixed with the antennaed Marhats. Its currency is the eron, backed by Euredium, a rare metal that regenerates.

Its sport is rasso: nine players a side carry a ball while surfing toward a finish line, a mix of American football and tennis played on water.

Ralu, whose name is also the number seventeen, is the almighty creator. Legend says he lived 171717 years ago, was thrown by seven traitors into the black flames of Doros, and ascended. The scriptures are the 17 books of Hanor.

Language and life go together. At weddings the bride cuts her left palm and the groom his right, and they clasp hands so their blood mixes as they swear their vows.

Hospitality has a taste too: ghamasso, potatoes baked in an egg, milk and cheese batter, served with grilled deer meat. Darleks, the pet birds whose spread wings look like living kaleidoscopes, stay out of the kitchen.

A formal greeting for guests: Esohar. Ahaeret mue laruen desa malhar deroset avlaset elrohim belhar belharet malor Aranor belhet. It means: greetings, friends, welcome to our dear ancient kingdom, protected by the gods and kept by eternal flames.

Your first week

  1. Day one: greet someone with Esohar and say Dasdon when they reply.
  2. Day two: learn the numbers to ten.
  3. Day three: use emue to say who you are, and ask qet (what) or qao (where).
  4. Day four: add -en to make plural nouns, then post three in the chat.
  5. Day five: write one sentence about fire (aranor) or stars (maron).
  6. Day six: read your sentence aloud in voice night.
  7. Day seven: translate a phrase for the next newcomer.
